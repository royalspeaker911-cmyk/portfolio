Rear Wing Active

Adjustable rear wing delivers up to 420kg of downforce at maximum speed. Track-tunable from the cockpit.

STO Drive Mode

Three driving modes — STO, Trofeo, Pioggia — each tuning suspension, traction and throttle response.
